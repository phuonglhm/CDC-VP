# Contributing to CDC-VP

CDC-VP accepts contributions under the repository's Apache License 2.0.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that:

- you created it or otherwise have the right to submit it;
- your employer or other rights owner has authorized the submission when
  required;
- it contains no confidential, proprietary, or export-restricted material
  that the project is not authorized to publish;
- third-party code and assets are identified with their source, exact license,
  copyright notice, and provenance; and
- the contribution may be distributed under Apache-2.0 without additional
  terms, except for clearly separated third-party material documented in
  `THIRD_PARTY.md`.

Do not commit downloaded toolchains, SDKs, model source, media datasets, or
other third-party binaries merely because they are publicly accessible.
Public availability is not proof of redistribution rights.

Every pull request that adds or changes third-party material must update
`THIRD_PARTY.md` and the relevant file under `licenses/`. Non-code assets must
also have an ownership or license record. See
`docs/PUBLIC_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`.
